# Delusional Business Inc.

A business simulation game for iPhone (web app). Start with a normal job, found any of 20 businesses, rent or buy property in Warsaw, London and New York, hire staff, and simulate day by day toward billionaire status.

**Play:** https://wik60.github.io/Buisness-INC/

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. It runs full-screen and offline. All save data stays on your device (localStorage); export/import a backup from Settings.

## Features
- 20 business types, each with its own economics (ticket size, margins, wages, how much foot traffic and skill matter)
- Property market: commercial parcels, offices and warehouses across 7 districts per city, with staff capacity and customer flow
- Warehouses required for Clothing, Supermarket, Furniture, Cosmetics, Construction and Delivery
- Hiring: Public Job Center (10 low-skilled hires/day) or Recruiters in your office (skilled candidates, 100 hires/day each)
- Worker skill 0–100 drives productivity, satisfaction, reputation and demand
- Office roles: Recruiter, HR Manager, Accountant, Marketing Manager
- Progressive income tax every 50 days
- Day job with promotions, random events, overdraft and bankruptcy

Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step.
